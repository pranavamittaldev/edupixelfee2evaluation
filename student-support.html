Frequently Asked Questions

Self Help

Q: How do I apply for Medical Attendance Exemption?

A: Navigate to "Requests & Applications" or "Leaves" module, upload doctor notes in PDF, and submit for Faculty Approval.

Q: Where can I download official semester fee receipts?

A: Go to "Fees & Payments" module under Treasury and click "Download Receipt" next to your confirmed transaction ID.
